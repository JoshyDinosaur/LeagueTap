LeagueTap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

LeagueTap ("we," "us," or "the app") is a companion app for Sleeper fantasy football leagues. This policy explains what information LeagueTap uses and how.

What we collect

LeagueTap does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect a name, email address, or password from you.

To use the app, you enter your existing Sleeper username. We use this to look up your public Sleeper leagues, rosters, and matchups through Sleeper's public API. This data is publicly available through Sleeper and is used only to power the features you see in the app (lineups, matchup breakdowns, and league-specific news).

Your current league selection is stored locally on your device (or in your browser) so the app remembers your preferences between sessions. This information is not shared with us or with any third party.

How we use it

League and roster data pulled from Sleeper is used to generate the content shown in the app, including AI-written recaps and start/sit breakdowns. Some of this content is generated using Anthropic's Claude AI models, which process the same public league and player data already visible in your Sleeper league — never anything else.

What we don't do

We don't run ads, sell data, or use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. We don't share your information with data brokers or marketers.

Children's privacy

LeagueTap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

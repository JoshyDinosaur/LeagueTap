Getting started

Open LeagueTap and enter your Sleeper username on the welcome screen. We'll pull in your leagues for the current season automatically — no password or separate account needed.

Switching leagues

If you're in more than one Sleeper league, use the league switcher in the app to jump between them at any time.

Common questions

Why don't I see my league?

Make sure the Sleeper username you entered matches exactly, and that the league is active for the current NFL season.

Is my Sleeper data safe?

LeagueTap only reads public league/roster data that's already visible through Sleeper — we don't ask for or store your Sleeper password.

Something looks wrong or out of date.

Content refreshes automatically throughout the day. If something looks off, email us below and we'll take a look.
